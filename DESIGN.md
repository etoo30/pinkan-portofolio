# Design System

## Surface

Pinkan Amalia Marzanda's one-page professional portfolio. Experience mode: the work and its evidence lead; navigation supports exploration.

## Visual direction

An editorial academic portfolio inspired by the quiet precision of a well-composed research journal. Ivory paper-like fields, navy ink, warm grey rules, confident serif display typography, and concise sans-serif reading text. The page moves between open editorial layouts, a distinct hero composition, a chronological experience rail, and denser skill groupings. It avoids the visual language of hospital software, pharmaceutical retail, and generic corporate templates.

## Tokens

- Ink navy: `#17263D`
- Ivory: `#F7F5F0`
- Beige: `#E8E4DC`
- Soft grey: `#A7A69F`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Body: `#42464D`
- Muted: `#777B82`
- Display: Cormorant Garamond
- Body and interface: Inter
- Content measure: 72rem maximum; body copy constrained to a comfortable reading measure.
- Corners: restrained, mostly square editorial surfaces with soft rounding on media and controls.

## Interaction

Sticky navigation, mobile menu, smooth anchor navigation, restrained in-view reveals, and a back-to-top control. All controls are keyboard reachable; motion yields to `prefers-reduced-motion`.

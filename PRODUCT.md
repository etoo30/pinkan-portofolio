# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, Tailwind CSS CDN, custom CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, as specified in the project brief.

## Users

Recruiters, pharmaceutical organizations, and academic institutions reviewing Pinkan Amalia Marzanda's background and looking for a way to contact her. This audience and its priority are taken from the project brief.

## Product Purpose

Present Pinkan's academic background, pharmacy related experience, research, competencies, and professional contact details as an online portfolio. Success means that visitors can understand her experience and reach her through verified channels. Derived from the project brief.

## Positioning

A personal academic and professional portfolio for a Bachelor of Pharmacy graduate with stated interests in clinical pharmacy, pharmaceutical care, and evidence based healthcare. No professional license is claimed.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a single page on desktop or mobile to review education, experience, research, competencies, and achievements, then follow a professional contact link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static frontend only; no backend, authentication, database, CMS, or API.
- Must remain usable from a local static HTTP server and static hosting.
- No fabricated credentials, results, testimonials, statistics, or affiliations.
- The owner is a Bachelor of Pharmacy graduate and aspiring pharmacist; no pharmacist license is stated.
- The experience dates, thesis title, honors, CV contents, and other source facts must be checked by the owner before publication where flagged in the brief.
- Domain purchase and hosting choice are undecided. Do not configure DNS or create accounts.
- A PDF CV was provided in the project root and is copied to the site's documents directory for the explicitly configured download.

## Brand Commitments

The brief specifies an editorial, minimalist, academic visual identity with ivory, navy, beige, and grey; serif display type and sans serif body type; English content; and the supplied name and verified contact links.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project brief with education, experience, research, skills, achievements, and contact facts.
- `CV ATS Terbaru Pinkan Amalia Marzanda.pdf`, supplied in the project root.
- No profile photograph was supplied. Do not substitute a stock or generated image as Pinkan.
- No thesis publication link or project media was supplied.

## Product Principles

- Keep every public claim grounded in supplied information.
- Make academic and professional evidence easy to scan.
- Keep contact paths direct and functional.
- Preserve a readable, accessible experience on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard operable controls, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced motion support.

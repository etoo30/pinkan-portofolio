I am a Bachelor of Pharmacy graduate from Universitas Diponegoro, with a strong academic foundation and a genuine interest in pharmaceutical care, clinical pharmacy, and healthcare development.

Throughout my undergraduate studies, I engaged in academic, organizational, and professional development activities. As a Laboratory Teaching Assistant, I gained experience in academic support, scientific communication, teamwork, and laboratory-based learning.

Undergraduate thesis · Clinical pharmacy research

The Relationship Between the Rationality of NSAID Use and Pain Management Outcomes in Post-Cesarean Section Patients at RSD K.R.M.T. Wongsonegoro.

Universitas Diponegoro

An observational analytical study using a retrospective cross-sectional approach, exploring medicine-use rationality and pain outcome records.

  • Evaluated NSAID use by drug selection, dosage, and patient suitability.
  • Examined pain outcomes using Visual Analog Scale (VAS) data.
  • Used statistical analysis to investigate the relationship between medicine-use rationality and therapeutic outcomes.
  • Reviewed scientific literature related to rational drug use.

Project summary only. No sample size, statistical result, or conclusion is stated here.

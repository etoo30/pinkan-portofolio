abstract Open repository record

Undergraduate thesis · Clinical pharmacy research

The Relationship Between the Rationality of NSAID Use and Pain Management Outcomes in Post-Cesarean Section Patients at RSD K.R.M.T. Wongsonegoro.

Universitas Diponegoro

A retrospective cross-sectional study of post-cesarean patient records from January to December 2024. The UNDIP repository abstract reports a population of 730 patients and simple random sampling.

  • Assessed NSAID-use rationality using prescribing criteria, including drug choice, dose, and patient suitability.
  • Measured pain outcomes with Visual Analog Scale (VAS) data.
  • The abstract reports no statistically significant relationship between NSAID-use rationality and pain-management outcomes (p > 0.05).
  • It notes that other factors, including surgery duration, may influence pain outcomes.

Summary based on the abstract in the Universitas Diponegoro repository.
